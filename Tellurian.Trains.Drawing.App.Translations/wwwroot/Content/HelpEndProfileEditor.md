# Edit

The end profile is the shape of a module's end: its outline, the position and height of the tracks, and the screw holes. The board fitted at the end of a module is the end plate; it is cut and drilled to the end profile, and many modules share the same end profile.

Draw the end profile as seen from outside the module, with rail top at height 0 and the track centre at 0.

## Tools

- **Select**: click to select, drag to move. Shift keeps the move straight, Alt turns snapping off.
- **Upper edge**: click the points of the upper edge from left to right. Enter finishes, Backspace removes the last point and Escape cancels. With **Arcs** ticked, the next parts are arcs that continue smoothly from the part before. The new upper edge replaces the old one.
- **Track**: click where a track goes; its rail top is always at the rail-top line.
- **Screw hole**: click where a screw hole goes; it snaps to edges, other holes and the grid.

The line at the bottom of the pane tells what the chosen tool does.

## Buttons

- **Undo** and **Redo**, also with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
- **Delete** removes what is selected, also with the Delete key.
- **Mirror screw holes** copies the screw holes to the other side of the centre line, so that they are symmetric. Holes that already have a mirror image are not doubled.
- **Print** prints the end profile on a landscape page with its dimensions.

## Moving around

Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle mouse button or with Space held down to pan, and click the button in the drawing's upper right corner to see the whole drawing.

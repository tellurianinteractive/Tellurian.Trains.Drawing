# Edit

The module is drawn from its exits, end profiles and track. In the editor you divide it into sections and change how it is identified.

## Tools

- **Select**: click a section joint or one of its points to select it, and drag to move it. Shift keeps the move straight, Alt turns snapping off. The ends of a joint stay on the module's edge.
- **Section joint**: click where the module is divided; a straight joint is added across the track there. The tool can't be used when the module has no outline.

The line at the bottom of the pane tells what the chosen tool does.

## Buttons

- **Undo** and **Redo**, also with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
- **Delete** removes the selected joint or point, also with the Delete key.
- **Print** prints the module drawing alone with the module's name, fitted to a landscape page.

## Moving around

Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle mouse button or with Space held down to pan, and click the button in the drawing's upper right corner to see the whole drawing.

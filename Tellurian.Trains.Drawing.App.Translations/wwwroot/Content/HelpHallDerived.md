# Derived properties

These values are calculated from the drawing and change as you draw; they can't be typed.

- **Size**: the width and depth of the hall.
- **Floor area**: the area inside the walls.
- **Effective floor area**: the floor area without pillars, furniture, the areas kept free in front of doors and no-go zones.
- **Walls**, **Obstacles**, **Doors and exits** and **No-go zones**: how many there are.
- **Module entrances**: the doors where modules can be carried in and out.

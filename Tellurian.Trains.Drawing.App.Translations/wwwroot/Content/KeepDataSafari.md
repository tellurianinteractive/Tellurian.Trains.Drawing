# Keep your data in Safari

Safari deletes the data of a website you haven't used for seven days, including your settings and drafts. To keep them:

1. On a Mac, choose **File** › **Add to Dock** and use Module Draw from the Dock.
2. On an iPad or iPhone, tap **Share** › **Add to Home Screen** and open Module Draw from the Home Screen.

Safari keeps the data of apps added this way. Everything in a private window is deleted when it closes.

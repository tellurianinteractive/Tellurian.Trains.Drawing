# Properties

Identify the module by the owner's FREMO prefix and a FREMO number, by a name, or by both.

- **Owner's FREMO prefix** and **FREMO module number**, e.g. *SFjä 012*. A FREMO number needs the prefix.
- **Name**: needed when the module has no FREMO number.
- **Owner's full name**: the owner may be someone other than you.

The FREMO designation, or else the name, is written on the module along the track, with the owner's name in smaller text under it. Problems are shown in red but don't stop you, so the fields can be filled in in any order. Changes can be undone.

The **Revision log** shows who saved the module, and when.

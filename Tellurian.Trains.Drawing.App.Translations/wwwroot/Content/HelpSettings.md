# Settings

Your settings are kept in this browser, never in drawing files.

- **User name** is recorded in the revision log of the drawings you save.
- **Country** and **Scale** decide which standards and end profiles are offered.
- **Module Registry API key** is for the Module Registry, when the app uses it.

## Keeping your data

*Storage in this browser* shows whether the browser keeps your settings and drafts, and how to make the browser you use keep them. Export your settings to a file, and import them again if the browser has forgotten them or in another browser.

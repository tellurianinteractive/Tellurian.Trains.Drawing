# Outline

The *Outline* pane gives the end profile's upper edge point by point, with exact measures in millimetres. The sides go straight down from the first and last points to the bottom edge. The upper edge is also the ground line that is compared where modules meet.

## Measures

- **Measure from**: rail top at the centre, or the end profile's lower left corner. All measures in the editor, the dimensions in the drawing and the printout follow this choice. Y is negative below rail top.
- **Dimension screw holes and points** adds a dimension for each screw hole and point, in the drawing and in the printout.
- **Bottom below rail top** sets the bottom edge. The **width** follows from the first and last points.

## Points

Each row is a point of the upper edge, from left to right. Type its X and Y, or drag the point in the drawing. Click the number to select the point.

**Type** tells how the edge reaches the point:

- **Line**: straight to the point.
- **Arc**: a circular arc to the point. The row below shows the arc's centre, which you can type, and its radius.
- **Fillet**: rounds the corner with the radius R. The centre lies on the side that fits best, or choose above or below. The row below shows where the fillet ends.

**Add point** adds a point after the selected one, or in the middle when none is selected. **Delete point** removes the selected points; the upper edge always keeps at least two.

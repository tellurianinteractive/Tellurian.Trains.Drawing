# Keep your data in your browser

Your browser may delete your settings and drafts, e.g. when it closes or when disk space runs low. If they disappear:

1. In the browser's privacy settings, look for a setting that deletes cookies and site data when the browser closes.
2. Add this site's address as an exception that is allowed to keep its data.
3. If the browser can install websites as apps, install Module Draw: browsers keep the data of installed apps more reliably.

Everything in a private window is deleted when it closes.

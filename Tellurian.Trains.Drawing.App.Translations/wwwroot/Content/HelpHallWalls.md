# Walls

The *Walls* pane gives the hall's corners with exact measures in metres; you can also type e.g. 450 mm. For a rectangular hall that isn't turned, you can type its **Width** and **Depth** directly.

## Corners

Each row is a corner and the wall from it to the next corner; the last wall goes back to the first corner. Click the number to select the corner.

- **X** and **Y**: where the corner is.
- **Length**: the length of the wall. A new length moves the corner where the wall ends. The length of a curved wall can't be typed.
- **Arc**: 0° for a straight wall. Another angle makes the wall curved; positive and negative angles curve it to opposite sides.

**Add corner** adds a corner in the selected wall, or in the last wall when none is selected. **Delete corner** removes the selected corner; a hall always keeps at least three.

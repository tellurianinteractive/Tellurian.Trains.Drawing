# End profile

With nothing selected, the pane shows the end profile itself:

- **Designation**: its name in the catalogue, e.g. FREMO-H0-B96.
- **Short name**: written along the module's end in module drawings, e.g. B96. Without it, the designation is used.
- **Scale** and **Standards**: given when the end profile was created; they can't be changed here.
- **Screw-hole pattern**: the name of the pattern the screw holes follow, if it has one.
- **Source document** and **Source URL**: the drawing or standard the measures come from.
- **Revision log**: who saved the end profile, and when.

## Selected items

Select a track, a screw hole or a dowel to change it; the pane then takes its name.

- **Track**: its distance from the centre, or from the left edge, as chosen in *Measure from* in the *Outline* pane; its gauge; and a label.
- **Screw hole** and **dowel**: the position of the centre, X and Y, and the diameter.

With several items selected, the pane shows how many.

# Edit

Draw the hall where the modules will be set up: its walls, pillars, furniture, doors and the areas that must be kept free. Measures are in metres; you can also type e.g. 450 mm.

## Tools

- **Select**: click to select, drag to move. Shift keeps the move straight, Alt turns snapping off. Drag a corner or a wall to reshape the hall; doors follow their wall. A selected item has handles to resize it; drag inside it to move it.
- **Walls**: click the corners of the walls, and click the first corner again to close them. The new walls replace the old ones.
- **Round pillar** and **Square pillar**: click where the centre of the pillar is; change its size in the properties.
- **Furniture**, e.g. a stage or a counter, and **No-go zone**, an area where nothing may be placed: click the corners, and click the first corner again to close.
- **Door** and **Emergency exit**: click on the wall where the opening is.

While you click corners, Backspace removes the last one and Escape cancels. With **Arcs** ticked, the next parts are arcs that continue smoothly from the part before. The line at the bottom of the pane tells what the chosen tool does.

## Snap grid

Points snap to a grid of the chosen size, 0.1 m unless you choose another, which makes rectangles easy to draw. With *Off*, the grid follows the zoom, and points snap to it only when they are close.

## Buttons

- **Undo** and **Redo**, also with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
- **Delete** removes what is selected, also with the Delete key.
- **Print** prints the hall on a landscape page, with its size and floor areas.

## Moving around

Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle mouse button or with Space held down to pan, and click the button in the drawing's upper right corner to see the whole drawing.

## Keyboard

The keys work wherever you are on the page, except while you type in a field.

- **1**–**8** choose a tool, in the order of the buttons. **0** shows the whole drawing.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes and **Ctrl+Y** redoes (⌘ on a Mac).
- **Delete** or **Backspace** deletes what is selected.
- **Arrow keys** move what is selected 1 mm, with **Shift** 10 mm.
- **Escape** stops what you are doing: first a drag or a shape you draw, then the tool (back to Select), then the selection.
- **Ctrl**+click adds to the selection.

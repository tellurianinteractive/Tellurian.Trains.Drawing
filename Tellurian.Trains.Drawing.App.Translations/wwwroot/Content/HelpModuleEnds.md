# Ends

The *Ends* pane shows each end of the module and its end profile.

- **End A**, **End B**: choose another end profile of the module's scale, also of another standard, e.g. FREMO at one end and MMM at the other. The module is drawn again with the new end profile, and gets the width it gives.
- **Flip** turns the end plate the other side out, so that an asymmetric end profile's high side is on the other side of the track. Only asymmetric end profiles can be flipped.
- **Endewender**: ticked when the asymmetric end profile is on different sides of the track at the two ends. It follows when you flip an end; ticking it flips end B.

## Section joints

When the module is drawn again, its section joints keep their place along the track and their distance from the centre of the end plates, and its name and other properties are kept. With the tracks in the middle of the end plate, single or double track, the bends of the joints stay as they are. When an end profile has its tracks off the centre, the bends move relative to the tracks, and you are asked before the change.

**Flip curve** in the *Edit* pane makes a curved module turn the other way; its joints are mirrored with it.

Only modules drawn from a template can get another end profile or curve; the ends of other modules can still be flipped. Every change can be undone.

# Keep your data in Edge

Edge keeps your settings and drafts unless it is set to clear site data when it closes. If they disappear:

1. Open **Settings** from the … menu and choose **Privacy, search, and services**.
2. Under **Clear browsing data**, choose **Choose what to clear every time you close the browser**.
3. If **Cookies and other site data** is on, click **Add** under **Don't clear** and paste this site's address.
4. Installing Module Draw as an app, under **Apps** in the … menu, makes Edge keep its data more reliably.

Everything in an InPrivate window is deleted when it closes.

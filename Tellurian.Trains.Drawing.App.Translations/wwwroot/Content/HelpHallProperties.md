# Hall

With nothing selected, the pane shows the hall itself:

- **Name**, **Address** and **Note**.
- **Minimum aisle width**: the narrowest aisle that hall plans in this hall should leave. 0 uses the minimum clearance of the hall plan's standard.
- **Revision log**: who saved the hall, and when.

## Selected items

Select something in the drawing to change it; the pane then takes its name.

- **Obstacle**, a pillar or furniture: its kind and label. A round one has its centre and diameter; a rectangular one has its centre, width, depth and rotation.
- **Door**: door or emergency exit, label and width. Tick *Modules can be carried in and out here* for the doors modules are brought through; they get a blue double arrow. Choose the wall, and type the distances along the wall from the corners to the edges of the opening. *Keep free in front* is how far into the hall the area in front of the door must be kept free.
- **No-go zone**: the reason nothing may be placed there.
- **Corner**: X and Y. **Wall**: length and arc, as in the *Walls* pane.

With several items selected, the pane shows how many.

# Welcome to Module Draw

## General

Module Draw is for drawing model railway end profiles, modules and hall plans for module meetings.

This is a pre-release for trying out. You can draw end profiles and halls, and create modules from templates. Editing modules and hall plans comes later.

Drafts are saved automatically in this browser while you work. Save your drawings to files to keep them and to share them.

Module Draw works offline and can be installed as an app.

## Browser support

Use a desktop computer with a Chromium-based browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi or Opera, or Chromium on Linux. In these browsers Module Draw opens and saves your files directly.

Firefox and Safari work too, with limitations: they can't save straight back to the file you opened, so saving is a download. Depending on the browser's settings, the file goes to your downloads folder or you choose where to save it; choose the original file to replace it. Recent files and folders must be picked again to be opened.

Your settings and drafts are kept in the browser. Browsers keep them unless their privacy settings delete site data, e.g. when the browser closes, or Safari after seven days without use. *Settings* shows whether your browser keeps them and how to change that in the browser you use, and lets you export your settings to a file and import them again.

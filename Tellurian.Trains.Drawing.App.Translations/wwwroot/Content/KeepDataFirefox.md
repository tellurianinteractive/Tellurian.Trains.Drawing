# Keep your data in Firefox

Firefox keeps your settings and drafts unless its privacy settings delete them when it closes. If they disappear:

1. Open **Settings** from the ☰ menu and choose **Privacy & Security**.
2. Under **Cookies and Site Data**, click **Manage Exceptions…**, paste this site's address, click **Allow** (not *Allow for Session*) and then **Save Changes**.
3. Under **History**, if Firefox is set to **Never remember history**, choose **Remember history**. With **Use custom settings for history** and **Clear history when Firefox closes**, the exception in step 2 is enough.
4. When Firefox asks whether this site may store data in persistent storage, choose **Allow**.

Everything in a private window is deleted when it closes.

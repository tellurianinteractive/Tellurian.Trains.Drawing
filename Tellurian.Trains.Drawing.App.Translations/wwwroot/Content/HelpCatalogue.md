# Catalogue

The catalogue holds the standards, add-ons, end profiles and compatibility overrides that the app offers when you draw.

- **Import files…**, **Import ZIP…** and **Import folder…** bring in end profiles; you choose for each one whether to import it.
- **Export your catalogue…** saves the catalogue to a file, to keep or to share.
- Problems in the catalogue are shown in the top bar; click one to correct it.

Which entries are offered depends on your country and scale in *Settings*.

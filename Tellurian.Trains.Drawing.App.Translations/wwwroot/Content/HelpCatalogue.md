# Catalogue

The catalogue holds the standards, add-ons, end profiles and compatibility overrides that the app offers when you draw.

- **Fetch from the Module Registry** brings in the Registry's standards and end profiles for your scale and country. End profiles the Registry has no drawing of are marked *no drawing*: **Create drawing** draws one for it, which keeps its designation and Registry reference when you save it to the catalogue. Until then it can't be used on a module.
  An end profile you have drawn yourself, with the same designation, standard and scale as one the Registry has no drawing of, becomes the Registry's: it keeps your drawing and takes the Registry's identity, and modules that use it are moved to it when you open them.
- **Import files…**, **Import ZIP…** and **Import folder…** bring in end profiles; you choose for each one whether to import it.
- **Export your catalogue…** saves the catalogue to a file, to keep or to share.
- Problems in the catalogue are shown in the top bar; click one to correct it.

Which entries are offered depends on your country and scale in *Settings*.

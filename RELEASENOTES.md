# Release Notes

## Release 0.2.0
Released 2026-11-14
- First public pre-release, for trying out. Drafts are kept in your browser; save your drawings to files to keep them.
- End profile editor: draw the end plate's upper edge from a dimensioned drawing with lines, arcs and fillets, place tracks and screw holes, with automatic dimensions measured from rail top or the plate's lower left corner.
- Print an end profile on a landscape A4 page, with dimensions and the title "Standard Scale Designation".
- Catalogue of standards and end profiles (FREMO, MMM), with your own standards, add-ons and overrides; import end profiles from files.
- Create modules from templates and view end profiles, modules and halls.
- Works offline and can be installed as an app; texts in English, Swedish, German, Danish and Norwegian.
- Known limitations: the module, hall and hall plan editors are not ready yet. The file format may still change before version 1.0. Saving and opening files works best in Chrome or Edge.
